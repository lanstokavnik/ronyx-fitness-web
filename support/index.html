Common questions

How do I get the app?

Ronyx Fitness is in testing with a small group of friends through TestFlight. Ask for an invite with the email of your Apple Account and we'll add you. You need an iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app is free. AI features use Claude: either with your own Anthropic API key, which Anthropic bills you for, or with shared access if we've approved you, which comes with a monthly limit.

Do I need an account?

Only for friends: comparing daily scores and sharing meals. Everything else works without one. Sign in with Apple or with a code we email you, in Settings → Account.

How do I add a friend?

Open the Friends tab and share your link, show your QR code, or type your friend's code. They accept the request and you both see each other's daily score.

How is the daily score worked out?

It's a score out of 100 from four parts: food against your targets, water, sleep and training against your plan. [[A part you haven't set up is left out rather than counted as zero, and a day with nothing logged shows no score.]]

My watch workouts don't show up.

Check that Apple Health is connected in Settings → Apple Health, and that your watch app writes workouts to Apple Health (in the Suunto or Garmin app, look for Apple Health in its settings). Suunto workouts can also be exported as FIT files and shared into Ronyx Fitness.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Pick a backup folder in iCloud Drive in Settings → Backups. On the new iPhone, install the app, sign in and choose Restore from a backup. Your API keys come over through iCloud Keychain.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single entries in the app, or delete your account in Settings → Account, which removes it from our server. Deleting the app removes everything on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for the details.

Is the coach medical advice?

No. Ronyx Fitness is for general fitness and nutrition tracking. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about health conditions, pregnancy, eating disorders or big changes to your diet.

Reporting a bug

Tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened, and add a screenshot if you can. In TestFlight you can also take a screenshot and tap Share Beta Feedback; it reaches us directly.
