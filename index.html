Coach

A coach that has read your week.

Ask what to eat now, how training went or why your weight stalled. The coach sees your logged food, workouts, sleep and targets, remembers what it learns about you, and answers with your numbers and a chart when it helps.

  • Send a photo of a menu and ask what fits your day.
  • A weekly check-in that adjusts your targets from what you actually ate and weighed.
  • Powered by Claude. Nothing is sent until you allow it.

Friends

Compete on the day.

Every day gets a score out of 100 from four parts: food, water, sleep and training. Add friends with a link or a QR code and see who had the better day.

  • Friends see your name, picture and daily score. Never what you ate or what you weigh.
  • Ate together? Share the meal and split it like a bill: in full, evenly or by percent. It lands in their log.
  • Notifications for friend requests, shared meals and workouts that came in.

And the rest of the day.

  • Water and electrolytes

    One tap per glass, from the app, the widget or the Action Button.

  • Phases

    Lean down, build, endurance or general health, with targets worked out from your body and goal.

  • Weekly report

    Your week as a short deck you swipe through, with a question to the coach on any card.

  • Plan tomorrow

    Build tomorrow from foods you already eat, then confirm each meal with a tap.

  • Energy balance

    What you ate against what you burned, corrected each week by your real weight trend.

  • Finish your day

    An evening reminder when something is still missing, so the score counts the whole day.

  • Daily backup

    A copy of everything once a day, to a folder you choose on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your iPhone's appearance, in the Ronyx look.

Privacy

Your log stays on your iPhone.

Meals, photos, weight, workouts and sleep are stored on your phone. Backups go to a folder you pick.

  • The server holds littleYour account keeps your email, name, picture and daily score. A meal you share waits there until your friends answer, 7 days at most.
  • You choose what Claude seesAI features ask first and send only what the feature you used needs.
  • No ads, no trackingNo analytics, no advertising and no selling of data. Delete your account in the app at any time.
